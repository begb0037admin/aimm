DESIGN MOCKUP — NOT LIVE CODE — Jules, 2026-09-05 (v2, revised same day) What this shows: the Mix Check cold-load / no-file empty state. Revised direction: Kevin sent a reference screenshot (different, unrelated product — the PATTERN is adapted here, none of their branding/copy carried over) that supersedes the original "lightweight banner only" framing — he wants a proper first-run guided tour with real presence, not just a subtle inline hint. Toggle First-run tour (now the primary treatment), Empty — today, Empty — after tour and Loaded below.
  • Kevin's original complaint (2026-09-05): "I understand it's blank... but there's no welcome from Hope. There's no introduction. There's no help tab where things point like 'click here first'... It's just blank."
  • The reference pattern, adapted for AIMM: a modal titled around Hope herself (not generic "the platform" language — she narrates in first person, in character), a horizontal step-roadmap strip (icon + label per stage, current highlighted, connected by lines), a detail card below showing the current step in depth (icon / "STEP N OF 5" eyebrow / heading / one-sentence Hope narration), Back/Next bottom-right, "Skip tour" bottom-centre, × dismiss top-right.
  • 5 steps, mapped to what Mix Check actually does (not forced to the reference's 4 or 7 — AIMM's real loop maps cleanly to 5): Upload → Measure → Fix Queue → Ask Hope → Confirm. See the tour itself for the exact per-step copy.
  • First-run only, never repeating — simulated here via a mixcheck_tour_seen_v1 localStorage flag (real key TBC with Cat); once dismissed (Next-through or Skip) it does not reopen on reload. Manually re-triggerable any time from the "?" icon next to Genre/Target/Settings in the Mix Check header — click it in any non-tour mode below to replay.
  • The original lightweight pass is kept, reweighted to secondary/ambient — the getting-started strip, Hope's rail greeting, and the centred Spectral Balance idle hint (see "Empty — after tour") are what the page rests on every time the tour isn't open (first visit before opening it, every repeat visit after it's dismissed, and manual replays). That's what keeps Kevin's original "never blank" ask true beyond just the first session, without the tour re-appearing and becoming heavy-handed.
  • Checked against docs/mockups/multistem-mixcheck.html (approved concept, not yet built) — the tour's Upload step and the getting-started strip both say "master (or stems)" deliberately; nothing here hardcodes single-file-only language.
  • Scope: this mockup does not touch Hope's actual conversational engine, ElevenLabs instructions, or voice behaviour (Markey's territory) — every line of tour/greeting copy here is a proposed static string design, not a change to how Hope actually speaks or reasons live. Flag to Markey before wiring any of this copy into her real voice/persona pipeline.
