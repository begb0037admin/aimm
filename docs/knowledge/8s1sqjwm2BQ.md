# Neal H. Pogue Mixing '10%' by Kaytranada ft. Kali Uchis

## Chunk 1

I'm Neal H. Pogue, and I'm here with Mix With The Masters. I'm here at The Mix Room Studio B and I'm going to show you what I did with a Grammy-winning song recently, called "10%", by an artist named Kaytranada featuring Kali Uchis. Sometimes people ask me, "How'd you get that bass to work with the kick?" It's about finding that right frequency so they stay out of each other's way. Sometimes you got to make that kick dirty so that the frequency in the bass can shine through, and this kick here has a little bit of distortion. I like the mix where things are dimensional, where it's not just right there in your face like one big wall. I want to create these levels. Sometimes, something has to take a back seat. I used the Maximizer just to open it up more, just give it a little more beef, a little more heaviness. And for this one, it was light. It's not heavy.
