## Chunk 6

So, I would have the drums dirt mute right on the downbeat. So, if you're going to set up something like a drums dirt, then I would highly recommend paying a lot of attention to the mute automation on it. It may seem counter-intuitive to turn it off on the very last kick drum because that kick drum will be quieter, right? It won't have all the parallel distortion and things like that. But it's much more distracting to have that kick drum have it and then you switch it off so you don't get the cymbals ringing because you hear the cymbals go and they shut down. And that really sticks out in the mix. So, if you mute it on that last kick drum and that last kick drum sounds weak for some reason, you can always automate the kick itself, but don't let things hang and chop the cymbals unless you're really paying a lot of attention to it as you do the mute automation.
