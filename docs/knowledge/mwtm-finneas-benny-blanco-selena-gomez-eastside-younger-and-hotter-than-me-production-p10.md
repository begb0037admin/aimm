## Chunk 1

I always say it's like the Selinas, the Rihannas, it's like the second their voice touches something, like Phineas has a great voice, but the second one of these, you're just like, oh. Well, they have, it's Rihanna and Selina, they have this thing that's like immediately, you're like, oh, like they wrote every word and they've been thinking these things for years. They all have that thing that's like interpretive thing. Yeah, and you just believe that. It's the same thing when I say like Phineas could play chords and then I could play the exact same chords, but they're not going to have the feeling or timbre. There's something about working at Phineas's studio. He has a piano, it's just every, any way he touches it, it sounds like magic instantly. He instantly does it and he's one of the most talented people I've ever worked with in my entire life.

## Chunk 2

It's like every time I'm in the room, I'm just like, please don't let this be the last time. Please don't let this be the last time. He has such a way of words and connecting. You know, Selina's story is so specific and he, he has such a way of just getting into her head and seeing what she wants to say and how she wants to say something. And she would tell me and we would go through it. And basically this album is kind of like diary entries of her life and my life.

## Chunk 5

## Chunk 10

And it's like, it's supposed to be creative and fun. People put so much pressure on themselves. I fall victim of it sometimes too. And it's like, we're making something we love. I would do this if it was still my passion. I did it after work. I would do it like the same way as I did as a kid.
