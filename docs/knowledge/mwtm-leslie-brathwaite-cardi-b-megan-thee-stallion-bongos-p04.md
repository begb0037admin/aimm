## Chunk 2

I think it's important to kind of figure out if that balance is there, especially when you're dealing with two females on the same song. Their whole focus is going to be, I'm telling you, this is the psychological game. The minute I turn this mix in, the first thing Cardi's going to be thinking about is, is Meg louder than me. And the first thing Meg is going to be thinking about is, is Cardi louder than me. So I got to make sure I get that right more than anything else when I'm, when I'm thinking about turning this mix in, is that particular, I will spend probably a half hour just sitting here bouncing back and forth between their vocals to make sure nobody's louder than the other person. Because the egos come into play when you've got two pretty major artists on the same record. I got to make sure they feel comfortable about being on the record with the other person. And that's really, you know, the thought process I enter into at this point.

## Chunk 4

## Chunk 9

And I will A-B my mix with and without it so you can kind of get a sense of what it's doing. So, this is without. And then this is with. ## Chunk 10
