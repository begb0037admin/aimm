## Chunk 6

And then the 60 is all about kick drum and floor tom. One of the things I hear when I take it out, is there's a boxiness to these drums. And there are two different ways you can take care of it. Now, most of the time, I would use the de-esser on the omnichannel, and I would suck out some of that 400 to 500 hertz that's in there, that makes it feel boxy. What I've done here is to smiley face EQ around it. By bringing up the bottom and the top, I'm technically turning down the midrange.

## Chunk 7

So two totally different approaches to do the same thing. I would do the opposite of this now. I'd most likely be sucking out the mids, and then I would be doing a high boost and a low boost, probably. But I would take care of the boxiness before I ever even started boosting things. So, same thing sonically, in terms of what I'm going for, but a completely inside-out approach to a lot of my more recent mixes.
