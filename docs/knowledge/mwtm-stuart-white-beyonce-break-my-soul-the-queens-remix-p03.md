## Chunk 2

I'm looking at this chain and I probably made this in my mind before I put it on there so I'm thinking, okay, I want a long delay but I want it to be reverb-y. I want it to be more filtered than the lead. I want to have a lot of delay, therefore I know I need to compress it quite hard to keep it in check and keep it behind the lead to differentiate. So in my mind I'm thinking I need to filter, I need to delay, I need to compress, and then I want some saturation to give it a tonal difference. So I need some saturation, that could be decapitator or lo-fi as we see here, and then I'm side-chaining it to the lead itself so when she talks the delay goes back behind it and then comes out a bit.

## Chunk 3

So I have this compressed reverb-y delay with saturation and then I'm controlling it with side-chaining and then a final EQ for some additional tone chafing. And that's kind of how I think about it. Even looking at it now, I put the compressor before the saturation and that's for a reason. Distortion and saturation is level dependent. So I need to compress it first so it saturates evenly because the level is more contained so the peaks are not going to distort more so I have an even amount of saturation. That's the thought process of why I would do that before this.
