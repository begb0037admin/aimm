Most of the chord data is actually just in her voice. The Little AlterBoy plug-in is so good at sort of making a voice feel very granular. That texture, to me, is so satisfying to add to a vocal moment. It can introduce a frequency range that a voice might sort of otherwise not be able to be capable of. It's fun to sort of artificiate that. Throw some Sample Delay, throw some Clip Distortion on it, throw some Valhalla Room, some Channel EQ, Little AlterBoy. I think, to me, that's the satisfying thing, when you take something where you're like, "Okay, this is what it looks like when you don't dress it up," and then when you dress it up, it takes on so much character.
