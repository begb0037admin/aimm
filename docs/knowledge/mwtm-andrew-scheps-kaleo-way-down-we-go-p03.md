## Chunk 1

Drums Dirt is something that I use as sort of a lengthener and a thickener for drums. So it's distortion. And it's really heavy distortion. And what I'll do actually, because it's sort of fun, is let me make this pre-fader so I can mute the source and you can hear what's going on. It is lo-fi at the beginning, doing a tiny bit. There's no point to it whatsoever. When I was starting to try and build this, it was because I was mixing a song. The drums sounded dry. It was just kick, snare, and there was no noise in between, no feel of a drum kit. I tried reverb, I tried crushing some of the other microphones, and nothing worked. It just didn't sound good. And so it occurred to me that distortion makes stuff have a decay. Because it's so busy distorting, it doesn't shut up right away. And so I thought, great, well, I'll try and build something out of distortion.

## Chunk 3

Then, there is a filter. I believe this is post, but I'm not even positive. Somewhere here you can see what the order is. Or maybe this is fixed. Let's say it's fixed. So, this filter is coming post the distortion, and it's pulling out mostly 3.3k. Two reasons for that. One, it would be super harsh on distorted drums.
