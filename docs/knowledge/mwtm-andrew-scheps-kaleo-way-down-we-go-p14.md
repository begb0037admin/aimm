## Chunk 13

One of the things I will say, though, there was a different record I was mixing while I was using this. Sometimes you can get inter-modulation distortion if you have just a couple of elements and they're slightly out of tune or they're in thirds. Because what will happen is with the harmonics of those you'll actually get beating, and that can be very slow. It can be one or two hertz. With a really fast release time, the limiter will start limiting based on this little rubbing between the notes, and it will flutter, and it's distortion, and it took me forever to find it. It never occurred to me that the distortion between a lead vocal and a harmony vocal was being caused by the limiter. So, if you like to work with fast release times on processing, know that that's a place to look if you're getting weird inter-modulation distortion. That's my tip of the day.
