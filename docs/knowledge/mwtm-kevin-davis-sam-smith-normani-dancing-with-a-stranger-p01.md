I'm Kevin K.D. Davis, otherwise known as K.D. I'm here with Mix With The Masters. We're going to go over a song by Normani and Sam Smith called Dancing With A Stranger. I started mixing in the studio or started as an assistant engineer in the studio when I was 19 right out of high school. Worked for a couple of studios around town for a couple of years and then one of the main studios I worked at was called Soundcastle. From there on I started getting clients as a recording engineer and started mixing people's records and then moved over to different studios around and gaining clients over the years and got a chance to mix on a bunch of different genres from rap to hip hop to R&B to pop, all kind of records.
