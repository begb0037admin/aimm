## Chunk 1

When I sit with the song, I'm just trying to figure out how to make every single moment move you forward. Every moment you hear something almost new. Every moment is complementing Abel's songwriting and feeling. Hey everybody, I'm Illangelo and I'm here at OCL Studios in Calgary, Canada. I'm really excited to be here sharing with you all a breakdown from The Weeknd's "After Hours" album. I really wanted to take a step out of looking at a song and instead almost visualize it like I'm in a movie or a whole new reality. Used a quarter-note delay, but the big difference is that the mode is switched into Pitch. So this is a really wonderful tool because now I was able to do the octave up. Now it's speaking back. Now it's a little bit more of a conversation between the initial performance and the delay. This Low End Focus is an incredible technology from Ozone and I found that putting it on Punch and increasing the Contrast, I just want the Middle channel where that kick drum is to be affected by this in combination with the rest of the sound. Now, as you guys can hear, that added a lot more of a defined focus on that kick drum. You can easily get stuck in that whole world of perfect intro, verse, pre, chorus. Keep in mind you all, we're making music. It's supposed to be resonating with us, with humans. Feel with the heart, listen to stuff. Don't look at things so structurally. You want to feel like you're in this world, you're captivated.
