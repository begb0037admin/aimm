## Boundaries and unresolved evidence

- The installed source has no readable `cached_checks.py`, `feature_flags.py`, `plugin_categories.py` or `login_launch.py`, although other modules import them. Static checker registrations, flag references and a saved cached report establish reference intent and historical output, **not** current service availability or known flag defaults.
- PluginHub's public share Worker, vendor sites, account/licence gates, current direct installer URLs and actual action timing were not invoked or verified in Phase 1. AIMM must define its own contracts in Phase 2.
- The manual and installed build differ in places. The current `profile_compare.build_list` comment says the share-dialog install-date checkbox was removed; retain the field in data compatibility, but do not invent a current UI control. This matrix prioritizes installed 2.0.12 behaviour and records manual-only items as such.
- Product counts from separate screenshots and scans are different observations. Their definitions and bounded reconciliation are in the Phase 1 evidence package. No target count should be hard-coded from one screenshot.
- Kevin excluded AAX and Pro Tools, but did not exclude Cubase. Cubase session-list support therefore remains in scope for parity.
