Notes

Heads up: voice needs a secure context (HTTPS or localhost). Chrome blocks the mic on file:// URLs — open this artifact via the Cowork sidebar, or serve the folder with python3 -m http.server and open http://localhost:8000. Safari is more forgiving but still needs mic permission.
How it works: your ElevenLabs key + Agent ID open a live conversation with Hope — ElevenLabs voice, Claude Sonnet 4.6 brain. She has tools for your chain, library, meters, insight notes, and flags, so she reads and edits the workbench while you talk. Try: "what's on my master bus?", "add Maag EQ4 after Ozone", "switch genre to R&B", or "I'm hearing pumping on the master."  At end of call your profile auto-extracts via Haiku so she remembers across sessions. Live transcript and snapshots show on the Hope tab.
